Personal History:
Zoe was born the youngest sibling to her family. As a child, she had already shown signs of being a bit different from most kids her age, both in terms of being very gifted and creative, and also by not resonating with her birth gender. Eventually, as she grew older, she would finally reveal herself as transgender to her family, and start her transition.

At the age of 20, Zoe and her sister created the first Zenith control mask, using it upon an unsuspecting delivery driver. With this newfound technology, her sister Clara wanted to use it to shape the world into her image, meanwhile Zoe wanted to use it more for weird kink stuff. As a result, the pair fought eachother until Zoe's dormant thermokinetic abilities became present, the attack causing her sister to have a permanent scar on the left side of her face.

Since this day, the two sisters had been at odds, with Clara taking the drone mask and improving it and adding Latex bodysuits to the ensemble, creating the Zenith Collective. Meanwhile, Zoe chose to start creating countermeasures to the Zenith, the two waging a neverending war for seven years, their battles becoming a common sight.

However, after being critically injured by a rail-cannon shot during a fight, Zoe had sculked her way from the fight barely clinging onto life. It was here that she met Aleena Borealis; The owner of a massive luxury resort for adults. The strange woman offered to heal Zoe's wounds and to bring out her more dormant abilities, initially for no payment. However, Zoe still wanted to offer something in return, so Aleena made her the new Carmine of the Borealis Upper Ring.

Since this day, Zoe (now under the alias "Carmine"), had been serving as the Upper Ring for the Carmine Sector, specialising in Bondage, Latex and Drones. With her most dormant power drawn out, her thermokinetic abilities now also include electrokinetic powers, as well as the spontaneous conjuring of two extra heads on each side of her body.

A female dragon and the older sister to Zoe. When they were young, the pair were inseperable as Clara was more timid and reserved than Zoe. As the pair grew older, they both grew a passion for electronics, but Clara also gained a concerning belief in "might makes right". While Clara had Zoe on the backfoot with her Toxikinesis, Zoe had somehow awakened her dormant thermokinetic abilities, a stray flame scarring Clara permanently.
