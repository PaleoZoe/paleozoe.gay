Personal Details:

Personal History:
Zoe was born to a small family of Novaerians in one of their last remaining colonies. She had a sister and two parents, and she came out as trans and socially transitioned until the age of 11, where she was abducted by Terrans, after they had lost a war to them caused by the Novaerians wanting to settle near earth.

For the next 15 years of her life, she was used as a living weapon by the Terrans, her flame breath being a key asset to them. For these years, she faced brutal humiliation, mockery and even abuse by the terrans, until the ship she was imprisoned on was captured by Affini forces, losing her left wing as she tried to make a break for freedom during the chaos.

Once she was found by the Affini, Zoe was quickly "domesticated" by Serrulata, becoming her Floret and having a Haustoric Implant implanted into her neck. With her new life, she was able to develop more as a person, blossoming into the bubbly spark of joy she is today.
