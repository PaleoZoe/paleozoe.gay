Personal Details:

Personal History:
Cassidy, their birth name Cassandra Feyton-Nova, was born the youngest of the 7 children of the CEOs of the Feyton Mining company and Nova heavy refining. As the youngest child, they would never have inherited anything from the family outside of a cut of her parent's wealth. As a result, they were a rebellious child up till their ninteenth birthday.

As an young adult, they started to resent their family, wishing to exist for herself instead of having their family's name weighing over their head like a metaphorical Sword of Damocles. As a result of this and a falling out with their mother and father, they actively ran away from their home on LO-7, running to the planet that LO-7 orbited around Polymia.

After they ran away from home, they lived on the planet for a year until the Affini made their presence known, seizing the xenophobia and the start of the Terran Domestication War to fast track their identity change as they enlisted as a naval engineer

During the war, they had managed to get themself enlisted onto one of the key battlecruisers that made up the terran defence line; The Flame of Terra. While most of their days were filled with routine drills and improving their skills working on the weapons systems for the battlecruiser

Once the Affini had breached the defence line, the Flame of Terra immediately fled, but not before firing off some weapons on an Affini command ship. During this assault, Cassidy's right arm was crushed in the missile loading mechanism.

This incident, followed by their command's lack of care for Cassidy's wellbeing, started to simmer a deep disdain for their higher command and a deep feeling of being unwanted and disposable. This fully culminated in an incident a year after losing their arm, where they attacked the first officer using the cheap prosthetic arm they gave them for cutting down her medication and berating her performance, managing to leave the first officer blind in one eye and disfigured.

As they attempted to escape from the warship in an escape pod, they accidentally set off the ship's emergency beacon, alerting the ships presence to everyone in a few systems over... Including the Affini. As Cassidy managed to escape from the Flame of Terra, they were swiftly found by the Affini.

Name:

Age:

Relation:
Father (Disowned)
Description:
Cassidy's father and the CEO of the Feyton Mining Company. Before the Terran Domestication War, he had mining facilities on several systems across the middle rim of Terran space. Even before marrying the CEO of Nova Heavy Refining, he was already a trillionaire, capable of affording an entire mansion to himself and his family on LO-7. He held himself and his family name in high regard, something that drove him to odds with Cassidy. It was his abusive hand that forced them to run away, something he very much didn't care more

After the war ended and the terran people were domesticated, Markus desperately held onto his capitalist ideals, fleeing to some part of space in his own private star-yacht. After this point, while his yacht was later found, he himself was never seen again, the only thing out of place on the boat was small tufts of fur near his neatly placed shoes and one of the yacht's display set to Affini propaganda.

From detective examinations, it is assumed that he witness his wife under the effects of domestication, and chose to path of self-termination rather than to succumb to the Affini.

Description:
Cassidy's mother and the CEO of Nova Heavy Refining. Before the Terran Domestication War, She had control over a dozen large planet-wide refineries built into several planets in the middle rim of terran space. She chose to marry Markus Feyton after the pair met several times after a Gala on Ganymede station. She was more withdrawn from public life, usually allowing her husband to speak on her behalf. She was also very distant and cold with her children, leading all but one of them to become equally as bitter as they got roles in the two companies.

After the war ended, Felicity was one of the first terrans to seemingly embrace the Affini. However, this was mostly a farce, as she was discreetly funnelling weapons and supplies to feralist insurgents. However, her discreet dealings would eventually be found, leading to her involuntary domestication.

The last time she was seen in public, she was seen on an Affini propaganda broadcast, being used as a symbol for the failures of capitalism and the benefits of domestication.
