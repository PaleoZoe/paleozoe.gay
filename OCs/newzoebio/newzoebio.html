Personal History:
Zoe was born to a small rural family, living in the countryside. As she grew up, she knew there were parts of her that didn't fully aligned with what others like her were like; She didn't associate with many boys, and she didn't mind partaking in activities deemed "girly".

In time, this slowly blossomed into her realising she was a woman at heart, rather than a man. It took her a long time to come out to her family, but she started to slowly transition at the age of 26

At some point before her 27th birthday, she underwent an experimental procedure to have her mind transferred into a robotic body, one that kept her at the age of 27 forever, but also made her far more doll-like in appearance.

She didn't seem to mind this, infact said doll-like frame made her realise even more things about herself

Around the same time, she moved away from her family, seeking a life on her own with a few of her close friends
